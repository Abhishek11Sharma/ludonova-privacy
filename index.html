Privacy Policy

Ludo Nova · Android app · Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: Ludo Nova is a fully offline board game. It collects, shares and sells no personal data.

Information we collect

None. Ludo Nova has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings, in-progress game and statistics are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • The app does not request internet access; it never contacts a server.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
